Delete my data

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open KContract.
  2. On the home screen, tap Privacy and data.
  3. Tap Delete my data, then confirm.

Your first name, your games, your anonymous ID and your notification token are erased from the server right away. If a game is running, you leave it and it carries on without you.

Without the app

Write to bohohoindustries@gmail.com with the subject "KContract deletion", giving the first name you used and the approximate date of your last game. Deletion is carried out within 30 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

Everything about you is deleted. Nothing is kept, except what Google Play keeps on its side for your purchases, under its own rules.
