Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

KContract is a party game. It asks for no account, no email address and no phone number, and contains no advertising or tracking tools.

Data controller

Bohoho Industries, publisher of KContract. Contact: bohohoindustries@gmail.com.

Data we process

DataWhyHow long
The first name you type for a gameTo show it to the other players in that game7 days after the game ends
An anonymous ID created by the app on your phoneTo recognise you in your games without an accountUntil you delete your data
What happens in games: targets, missions, points, logTo run the game7 days after the game ends
A notification tokenTo send you game alerts (kills, events)60 days without use

Game preferences (vibe, penalties, language) and the status of your purchases are kept only on your phone.

Purchases

Purchases (Party Pass, lifetime access) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.

Hosting and processors

  • Supabase: the game database, hosted in the European Union.
  • Expo and Google Firebase: delivering notifications to your phone.
  • Google Play: app distribution and payments.

No data is sold or used for advertising.

Your rights

You can erase all your data at any time from the app: Home > Privacy and data > Delete my data. You can also ask by email, see the deletion page. Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Age

KContract is meant for adults. The "sips" mode refers to alcohol; the default mode is alcohol-free.
